Every direction reads the same previous state. A wave adds cells one step farther away. When no new cells appear, the region is complete.

04 / COMPARE THE FINISHED REGIONS

A completed line becomes territory. Then examine the remaining free regions.

As in the book: if the line creates more than one region, capture the smallest region without enemies. A tie chooses the last equal candidate in scan order. Other regions are excluded from this search, but do not become territory.

FOUR BOOLEAN LAYERS

The drawing changes.
The masks explain why.

The field keeps territory, unfinished trail, player and enemy layers separate. After closing a line, a temporary blocked mask starts from territory. Each complete region is added to blocked, so it is examined only once.
