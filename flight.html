THE BOOK’S FOUNDATION

The rules live
in the representation.

The aircraft’s 20 local vertices and 29 edges come from Module 21. Movement follows its forward and left vectors. The world transform scales, rotates, and translates the geometry. Projectiles leave the transformed nose; collision tests compare distances with sums of radii.

THE BROWSER EXPANSION

Three sectors, more ways to think.

Sector 1 uses the book’s six enemies and their diagonal and circular paths. Sectors 2–3 add armoured units, aimed projectiles, pursuers, and a command ship. Three shields and a short grace period make it easier to experiment. Enemy spin and movement remain separate quantities.

Pause. Predict. Test.

Pause, choose an enemy, and compare its local and world vertices. Turn your aircraft: its local shape stays the same, while the rotation matrix and forward vector change. Toggle collision circles to see the regions used by the game.

JavaScript adaptation and extension of the book’s 2D wireframe game. Python is not executed. Continuous collision checks between frames reduce missed fast projectiles. Screen coordinates use the canvas’s 768 × 768 drawing space.
