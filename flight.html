ONE FRAME, FOUR REPRESENTATIONS

The shape stays.
The coordinates change.

The aircraft is a matrix of local vertices and a list of edges. Steering changes its world transformation. The camera expresses the resulting points in its own coordinates; perspective divides x and y by depth.

Try a prediction

Pause, change the field of view, and inspect the selected vertex. Its world coordinates stay the same, while its screen coordinates change. A wider view makes the aircraft appear smaller.
